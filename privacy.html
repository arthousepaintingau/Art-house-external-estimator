PaintQuoteX External — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 21 August 2026

PaintQuoteX External (“PaintQuoteX”, “we”, “us” or “the Tool”) respects your privacy.

1. No Account or Login Required

PaintQuoteX does not require users to create an account or log in to use the Tool.

2. Information You Enter

The Tool allows you to enter information such as customer names, contact details, property addresses, quotation details, measurements, pricing and project notes.

This information is used only to provide the estimating, quotation and document-generation features of the Tool.

3. Local Storage

Quotation information is stored locally on the user’s device or web browser where applicable.

PaintQuoteX does not intentionally transmit or store this information on our servers.

4. Personal Information

We do not sell, rent or use quotation or customer information for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

PaintQuoteX does not require users to provide personal information to third parties in order to use the Tool.

Third-party websites or services accessed through external links may have their own privacy policies.

6. Your Responsibility

If you enter information about customers or other individuals, you are responsible for ensuring that you have the appropriate authority to collect and use that information.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be made available through PaintQuoteX.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact us at contact@arthousepainting.com.au.

9. Australian Privacy Law

This Privacy Policy is intended to operate consistently with applicable Australian privacy laws, including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) where applicable.
