PaintQuoteX Exterior — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 21 August 2026

PaintQuoteX Exterior (“PaintQuoteX”, “we”, “us” or “the Tool”) respects your privacy.

1. No Account or Login Required

PaintQuoteX Exterior does not require users to create an account or log in to use the Tool.

2. Information You Enter

The Tool allows you to enter information such as customer names, contact details, property addresses, quotation details, measurements, pricing and project notes.

This information is used only to provide the estimating, quotation and document-generation features of the Tool.

3. Local Storage

Quotation information is stored locally on the user's device. PaintQuoteX Exterior does not intentionally transmit this personal data to external servers.
